# Intent

Define and exercise trading systems through a common analytical foundation
that preserves the economics of the underlying subject and traded instrument.

The system makes a proposed action explainable in terms of its objective,
strategy thesis, evidence, feasible operations, expected outcome and risk.
It supports decisions under incomplete knowledge and revises those decisions
when observations or model assumptions change.

The scope includes physical commodities, currencies, equities and derivatives.
Oil supplies the first detailed domain and executable example. The product
serves systems analysis, model construction and decision support. It leaves
jurisdictional rules, corporate governance, permissions, risk appetite and
accounting presentation to independently identified overlays.

Success means that a reader can derive the implemented example from the
definition, reproduce its outcomes, challenge its assumptions and see a
materially different decision when consequential evidence changes. The PoC
tests the calculus, representation and composition interfaces. A simple
evaluator exercises those interfaces; developing an effective optimizer is
a separate undertaking. Lawful deal models, explicit tolerances and evidence
rules govern the represented trading system as its environment changes.
